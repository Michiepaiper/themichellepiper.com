THE 10 GOLDEN RULES
1
One message per screen.
Never overwhelm.

2
Whitespace is intentional.
Never fill space because it exists.

3
Every section must answer one question.

4
One primary CTA per screen.
Not three.

5
Every page begins with clarity.
No clever headlines that hide what we do.

6
Never use marketing jargon.
Speak like an expert.
Not a salesperson.

7
Use proof wherever possible.
Replace claims with evidence as The Michelle Piper grows.

8
Every image must support trust.
Not decoration.

9
Every page should teach something.
People trust brands that educate.

10
Always leave the visitor feeling more informed than when they arrived.

TYPOGRAPHY RULES
Never use ALL CAPS for body text.
Limit bold text to emphasis.
Avoid italics except for quotations.
Use sentence case for almost everything.
Line height: 150–170% (adjustable)
Paragraph spacing: 24px (adjustable)

QUALITY CHECKLIST
Before publishing any page:
Does it communicate value immediately?
Is the primary CTA obvious?
Are headings clear?
Is the copy concise?
Are trust signals visible?
Does the layout breathe?
Is it mobile-friendly?
Is it accessible?
Does it load quickly?
Does it reinforce The Michelle Piper's positioning?
If any answer is "no," the page isn't ready.
