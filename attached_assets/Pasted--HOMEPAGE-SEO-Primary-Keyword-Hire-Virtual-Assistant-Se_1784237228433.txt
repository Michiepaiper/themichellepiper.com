Frequently Asked Questions
Is the consultation really free?
Yes.
Our initial consultation is completely free and designed to help us understand your hiring needs.

How long does the consultation take?
Approximately 30–45 minutes, depending on the complexity of your hiring requirements.

Do I need to know exactly who I want to hire?
No.
Many employers know the problem they need solved but are unsure which role is the best fit.
We'll help you determine the right solution.

What happens after the consultation?
If we're a good fit, we'll explain the next steps, timelines, and begin the recruitment process.

Do you recruit internationally?
Yes.
We recruit professionals from Nigeria, South Africa, and Kenya for employers in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and Singapore.
